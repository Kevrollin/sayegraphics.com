# Information and photos for your new website

**For:** Saye Graphics Ltd, Moses Mudavadi Road, Nakuru

We have built a first version (a demo) of a website for Saye Graphics. It already includes your phone number, WhatsApp, location and Google rating, and a quote form that sends enquiries straight to your WhatsApp.

To make the site truly yours, we need a few things from you. You don't need everything at once: start with the items marked **Most important**. Photos from a phone are fine, as long as they are clear and well lit.

You can send everything on WhatsApp. For large files, please send them as **documents**, not as photos, so WhatsApp doesn't reduce the quality.

---

## 1. Your brand: most important

| What we need                | Best format                                  | Why                                             |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Your original logo          | SVG, PDF, AI or CDR; otherwise a large PNG    | Replaces the temporary "SG" logo on the site    |
| Your official brand colours | Colour codes (e.g. #2456E8) or a photo of printed material in your colours | So the whole site matches your brand |
| Brand guidelines, if any    | PDF                                          | Fonts, colours and logo rules                   |
| A slogan or tagline, if you use one | Text                                 | Can be used at the top of the website           |

## 2. Photos of your work: most important

Real photos of real jobs are what convince customers most. Around **10 to 20 photos** is ideal.

- **T-shirt printing:** finished T-shirts, laid flat or being worn
- **Hoodie printing:** finished hoodies, front and back prints
- **Graphic design:** posters, flyers, social media designs and anything else you have designed
- **Branding:** logos and brand identities you have created for customers
- **Your workspace:** the shop front, printing equipment, and work in progress

Please tell us if any photo shows a customer's brand or logo that you **do not** have permission to show online, and we will leave it out.

## 3. Your services and products

1. The **full list of services** you offer.
2. **Other products** you print or make (for example caps, mugs, banners, stickers or business cards), only if you offer them.
3. Is there a **minimum order** for T-shirts or hoodies? *(optional)*
4. How do customers usually **pay**, e.g. M-Pesa Till or Paybill? *(optional)*

## 5. How you work

1. What happens after a customer contacts you? (For example: they send the design, you share a sample or proof, they approve, then you print.)
2. **How long** does a typical job take? (For example: "T-shirts: about 2–3 days after approval.") We will only show timings you are comfortable with.
3. Do customers **collect** their order, or do you also **deliver**? If you deliver, where to?

## 6. Online presence

- Links to your **official** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or other pages, if you have them.
- Do you have, or want, a **web address** (for example www.sayegraphics.co.ke)?

## 7. Customer testimonials (optional)

Kind words from customers who have **agreed** to be quoted on your website: their name (or first name and town) and what they said. We will never publish a testimonial without permission.

### What we will never put on the website without your confirmation

Years in business, number of customers or projects, client names, awards, prices, guarantees, turnaround times or delivery areas.

Thank you! Once we have these, we will replace the sample designs with your real work and send you the updated website to review.
