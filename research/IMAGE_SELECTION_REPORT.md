# Image selection report

How the owner's photos and videos were chosen and placed. Asset IDs (P…, V…) refer to [ASSET_INVENTORY.md](ASSET_INVENTORY.md), which describes every file. Prepared 3 November 2026.

## What the uploads showed

The first version of the site described a business doing graphic design, branding and T-shirt/hoodie printing, because that was all the Google reviews mentioned. The 165 photos, 24 videos and the company profile show a much broader print and branding shop:

- **Signage:** 3D letters, illuminated and backlit signs, light boxes, neon, directional signposts, brass plaques, door and floor signs.
- **Large-format and outdoor:** banners, roll-ups, teardrop flags, event backdrops, billboards, street-pole banners, window and wall graphics.
- **Vehicle branding:** a full wrap (with before and after photos), ambulance livery, van lettering.
- **Branded apparel:** printed and embroidered T-shirts, hoodies, polo shirts, caps, reflective vests. A multi-head embroidery machine appears in both photos and video.
- **Promotional items:** mugs, bottles, pens, lanyards, umbrellas, clocks, key holders, wristbands, badges, bags.
- **Design and print:** logo design, business stationery (from the profile), flyers, calendars, labels, and laser-cut wedding invitations.

The services section was rebuilt around these six groups. Each is backed by the company profile, the service list on the business's own roll-up banner (P004) and photographed jobs.

## Main hero image

**Chosen: P043, the reception wall with a gold 3D logo and raised "Dashing Divas Beauty Parlour" letters.** It is cropped to 3:2 and leads a three-photo composition with P124 (embroidered "Kenya" hoodies) and P165 (teal and gold wedding invitations).

Why P043:

- **Visual impact and quality.** A gold mirror-acrylic logo and crisp white letters on a dark wall, evenly lit by two spotlights. It is the most polished, premium-looking photo in the set, sharp at 1280 px, with no clutter once cropped.
- **Brand relevance.** Interior signage is high-value work that tells a visitor at once that this is a serious branding company, not only a T-shirt printer.
- **Colour harmony.** Its gold and charcoal sit naturally with the brand's bronze (#8A5F35) and the site's ink and paper tones. The teal-and-gold invitations (P165) echo the brand teal (#336B86).
- **Crop flexibility.** The subject is centred with space on all sides, so a 3:2 crop removes the photographer's watermark (right edge) and a monitor (left edge) without touching the sign. It works at 350 px wide on phones and 600 px on desktop.

Why a composition rather than one photo: no single photo shows more than one service, and the brief asked the first screen to "communicate the business's creative and printing capabilities". Signage, embroidery and stationery in one frame does that. The composition sits inside the crop-mark frame the original design used for its illustrated artboard, so the layout and identity are unchanged.

## Videos

Four clips were selected for an "In the workshop" row in the About section. Each is a 4–7 second trim of the best moments, re-encoded at 360 x 640, 25 fps, silent:

| Clip | Source | Kept | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large-format printing | V16 | 0–2.4 s + 4.6–6.5 s | The shop's printer running, stock on shelves behind: real workspace |
| Logo embroidery | V10 | 5.6–9.8 s + 15.2–17.4 s | Machine heads stitching a logo, then the finished hoodie |
| 3D sign letters | V15 | 1.0–5.0 s + 21.5–24.5 s | Hands-on fabrication, ending on the full signboard |
| Installation | V19 | 15.5–21.5 s | A road-crossing banner going up with a crane truck |

How they behave:

- Each shows its poster image (the clip's first frame, about 10–27 KB) and downloads nothing until it scrolls into view.
- It plays muted and looping only while at least 40% visible, and pauses when it leaves the screen.
- With reduced motion or Data Saver on, it never starts by itself; the play button does.
- Every clip has a pause/play button.
- Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Android get VP9 WebM; Safari gets the H.264 MP4 fallback.

The hero has no video. Autoplaying video would compete with the headline and delay the first paint.

Not used:

- **V01:** the same Sakema sign as P009, but feet dominate the shot.
- **V05, V07:** TikTok watermarks.
- **V22/V23:** landscape clips of a rooftop sign, kept as candidates for a future landscape slot.
- **The rest:** shaky footage or text overlays.
